Install in a minute

Smart Tasks is installed as an unpacked extension while it waits for the Chrome Web Store.

  1. Download {{ZIP}} and unzip it. Keep the folder somewhere permanent, like Documents.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in your browser. In Comet use comet://extensions, in Edge edge://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode at the top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder. Pin Smart Tasks from the puzzle icon so it sits in your toolbar.

Updating: download the new version, replace the files inside your Smart Tasks folder, then click the reload icon on the extension card. Your tasks stay put. If you remove the extension instead, just sign in again and everything syncs back.
