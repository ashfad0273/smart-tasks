    Features

    Small, fast, and it just syncs

    Everything fits in a compact window, works offline and catches up when you are back online.

    Quick add that understands you

    Type "Pay rent friday 9am !high #home" and the date, priority and category fill in.

    Reminders that act

    Mark a task done or snooze it straight from the notification.

    Real-time sync

    Add a task at the office and it is on your home computer a second later.

    Works with AI agents

    Claude and other MCP apps can manage your tasks and send you notifications.

    One shortcut away

    The desktop app lives in your tray or menu bar. Press Ctrl Alt K from anywhere.

    Full history

    See what was added, finished or deleted, when, and whether you or your agent did it.

    Install

    Up and running in a minute

    Pick your platform for the steps.

    1. Run the installer you downloaded. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
    2. Smart Tasks opens and keeps running in the system tray. Press Ctrl Alt K anywhere to show or hide it.
    3. Sign in to sync with your other devices, or keep tasks on this computer only. New versions install themselves.
    1. Open the .dmg you downloaded and drag Smart Tasks into Applications.
    2. Open Smart Tasks. If macOS says it can't check the app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.
    3. Smart Tasks lives in your menu bar. Press ⌘ ⌥ K anywhere to show or hide it. New versions install themselves.
    1. Download {{ZIP}} and unzip it. Keep the folder somewhere permanent, like Documents.
    2. Open chrome://extensions in your browser. In Comet use comet://extensions, in Edge edge://extensions.
    3. Turn on Developer mode at the top right.
    4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder. Pin Smart Tasks from the puzzle icon so it sits in your toolbar.

    Updating: Smart Tasks tells you when a new version is out and installs it for you. Your tasks stay put, and if you ever remove the extension, sign in again and everything syncs back.
